One Number for today's overdraft

Health is not lost in a day. It erodes through small, repeated overdrafts — late nights, long sitting, skipped exercise, poor eating, overwork.

Health Debt doesn't judge, lecture, or gamify. It is a ledger. Objective, calm, and neutral — so you can see your patterns before they become problems.

Core Features

Everything a ledger needs.
Nothing a fitness app wants.

01 · Record

Log the overdraft, not the workout

Quickly record health-depleting behaviors with adjustable intensity. Each entry adds to today's total automatically — sleep late, sit too long, skip exercise, eat too late, overwork, and more.

7 today's health debt
02 · Today

Today's Health Debt

A single number on the dashboard — the cumulative health debt for today. No scores, no streaks, no celebrations. Just the overdraft.

03 · Ledger

A chronological health ledger

Browse records by day. Spot the behaviors that keep repeating, and the habits that keep draining future health.

04 · Reminders

Reverse reminders

Factual, low-frequency notifications when patterns suggest sustained or elevated debt. No motivational slogans. Facts only.

05 · Yours

Personalization without a profile

Custom behavior types, language, time format, and data export. No account required. You can clear everything from Settings.

06 · Private

Local by default

Every record stays on your device. We do not collect, transmit, or store your personal data on any server.

Data Insights

Trend charts, behavior rankings, and peak-time statistics — so you understand the source of the overdraft, instead of staring at a health score.

When it happens

Record the overdraft

Something depletes you — a late night, a long sit, a skipped run. Open the app, pick the behavior, set the intensity, record. That's the whole interaction.

The rest of today

One number on the home screen

Today's Health Debt sits in the middle of the dashboard. It does not cheer. It does not scold. It just tells you how much you already spent.

Looking back

Read the ledger

Days stack into a chronological account. Recurring behaviors surface on their own — the ones that keep charging interest on tomorrow.

Over time

See the pattern

Insights show totals, rankings, and when debt spikes. Health Debt does not try to change you. It helps you see — because only then is change possible.

Your data stays on your device

All health debt records are stored locally on your phone. We do not collect, transmit, or store your personal data on any server. No ads, no accounts, no cloud sync.
