Privacy Policy

Last Updated: 09/12/2026

This Privacy Policy ("Policy") explains how the developer of the "Health Debt" application ("Health Debt", "App", "We", "Us", or "Our") collects, uses, stores, and protects your information when you download, install, or use the App. By using the App, you acknowledge that you have read, understood, and agree to the practices described in this Policy.

1. Information We Collect
We are committed to minimizing data collection to protect your privacy. The App collects no personal data from you, except for the following information stored locally on your device:

1.1 Locally Stored User-Generated Data
All data you input into the App (collectively "User Data") is stored exclusively on your personal mobile device and is never transmitted to, collected, or stored on our servers or any third-party servers. User Data includes, but is not limited to:

• Health debt records you log (behavior type, intensity, date, and time);
• Custom behavior types you create;
• App preferences (e.g., language, time format, week start day);
• Data generated by the App based on your input (e.g., daily totals, trend charts, and behavior rankings).

We do not collect personally identifiable information from you (e.g., name, email address, phone number, browsing history, or location data). Limited non-personal technical information may be processed by Firebase solely for crash reporting, performance monitoring, and anonymous usage analytics, as described in Section 5. The App does not request notification permission or file-access permission for export or backup features.

2. How We Use Your Information
The only purpose for which information is used is to enable the App's core features—all processed locally on your device:

• User Data: Used to display your health debt ledger, calculate daily totals, generate insights and trend charts, and manage your custom behavior types.
• App Preferences: Used to personalize your experience (e.g., language and time display).

We never use your information for advertising, marketing, profiling, or any purpose other than supporting the App's intended functionality and improving its stability and performance (including through the limited Firebase services described in Section 5).

3. Data Storage and Security
3.1 Local Storage Only
As emphasized, all User Data remains on your device. We have no access to this data, and it cannot be accessed by us or third parties unless unauthorized access to your device occurs (see Section 3.2 for your security responsibilities).

3.2 Data Security
Your Responsibility: You are solely responsible for securing your device and the User Data stored on it. We recommend using device-level security measures (e.g., passcodes or biometric locks) to prevent unauthorized access.
Data Loss Risks: If you uninstall the App, reset your device, or experience device damage, User Data may be permanently deleted and cannot be recovered by Us.
No Cloud Sync: The App does not offer cloud sync functionality, meaning User Data cannot be recovered from external servers if lost from your device.

4. Sharing of Your Information
We never sell, rent, or disclose your User Data to third parties. Limited non-personal technical information may be processed by Firebase as described in Section 5. We may also disclose information in the following limited circumstance:
• Legal Requirements: If required by law, regulation, court order, or valid government request, we may be obligated to disclose information. However, because all User Data is stored locally on your device, we have no access to it and cannot retrieve it on your behalf.

5. Third-Party Services
The App may integrate with limited third-party services solely to support specific operational features. These services are not used to access or process your health debt records, which remain stored only on your device:

• Firebase (by Google): Used to support core app infrastructure, including crash reporting, performance monitoring, and anonymous usage analytics. Firebase may collect non-personal, aggregated technical information (e.g., device model, OS version, and crash logs) to help us improve app stability and performance. No personally identifiable information (e.g., name, email, or your health debt data) is collected or shared through Firebase.
• No Other Third-Party Integrations: Apart from Firebase as described above, the App contains no third-party advertising, social media plugins, or additional SDKs that collect user data.

We only work with third-party service providers that comply with widely recognized privacy standards (e.g., GDPR, CCPA, Apple's App Tracking Transparency guidelines, and Google Play Store privacy requirements).

6. Your Rights Over Your Information
Since all User Data is stored on your device, you have full control over it:

• Access: You can view all your User Data at any time through the App's interface (Dashboard, History, and Insights).
• Modify/Delete: You can edit or delete individual health debt records directly within the App. You can also clear all data from the Settings page.
• Permanently Delete Data: Uninstalling the App or resetting your device will delete all locally stored User Data.

To exercise these rights, no action is required from us—you can manage your data directly through your device or the App.

7. Children's Privacy
The App is not directed at children under the age of 13 (or the minimum age required in your jurisdiction). We do not knowingly collect any information from children. If we learn that a child has used the App, we cannot access or delete their data (as it is stored locally on their device)—we recommend that a parent or guardian uninstall the App from the child's device to remove the data.

8. Changes to This Privacy Policy
We may update this Policy from time to time to reflect changes in the App's functionality or applicable privacy laws. When we make changes:

• We will revise the "Last Updated" date at the top of this Policy;
• We may notify you by displaying a notice within the App upon your next launch.

Your continued use of the App after the updated Policy takes effect constitutes your acceptance of the changes. We encourage you to review this Policy periodically.

9. Compliance With Global Privacy Laws
This Policy is designed to comply with major global privacy regulations, including but not limited to:

• General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) (EU/EEA): Ensures your right to privacy, data minimization, and local storage of personal data.
• California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA) (U.S.): Guarantees your right to know, access, and delete your data, with no sale of personal information.
• Apple App Store Guidelines: Complies with Apple's requirements for data minimization and local storage.
• Google Play Store Policies: Adheres to Google's standards for privacy protection, transparency, and prohibition of unauthorized data collection.
